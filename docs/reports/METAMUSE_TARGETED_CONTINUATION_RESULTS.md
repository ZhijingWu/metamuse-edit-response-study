# Targeted MetaMuse-inspired continuation — 20 October 2026

**Result: NEGATIVE for the tested response-aware selectors.** The targeted
comparison is now complete at a 32-attempt search budget. Response information
does not improve fresh held-out discovery quality beyond strong cheap controls.
The current descriptor exactly ties neutral retention in conditional expected
test quality while costing **5.69% more**. Validity-aware repairs do not establish
an advantage. This is a result for one fixed parent bank and operator, not a
general impossibility claim.

The earlier [pilot report](METAMUSE_AUTHORIZED_LLM_PILOT_REPORT.md) remains
INCONCLUSIVE for its original interrupted 48-credit endpoint. It has not been
rewritten. All prior raw responses, programs, protocols, policies and negative
CPU results remain preserved.

**1. Is the comparison complete and fair?** Yes for the newly frozen,
32-attempt, one-retention-step comparison; the old 48-credit experiment remains
incomplete. The old matrix would need 52 unattempted requests to fill its
remaining slots, or 53 also to replace the failed slot. That would reuse future
outcomes already inspected while revising selectors. We instead retained the
old probe evidence, charged its original cost, and generated **64 genuinely new
future edits: eight per parent, four per edit label**. This is the minimum full
matrix supporting four retained parents and a 32-attempt zero-probe control.
A fresh 48-credit matrix would need 96 future edits.

Every selector receives the same eight human seeds, fixed training workloads,
mutation instructions, GLM-4.7 settings and fresh descendant matrix. Old future
children, including the known winner, are excluded from search. Revised
selectors were frozen before new calls and test evaluation. The new test has
**96 traces in eight independently seeded workload banks**; none duplicates an
old training/test item sequence. Model search still has only **one parent-bank
matrix**, not eight or 560 independent searches.

Two budget views were completed: 32 API-attempt/training-evaluation credits,
and the same ceiling with **CNY 0.062416** nominal generation cost and **30 seconds**
of measured training-evaluation time. Probe costs are charged, including the
old numerical failure; initial seed evaluation is common overhead. There is
**zero pool censoring** at the primary endpoints. Equal resource ceilings do
not imply identical spending at discrete request boundaries. Full money,
timing, unused-budget and event-prefix records are retained.

label-balanced future subsets and the random/neutral parent choices, rather
than pretending allocation replays are independent LLM experiments.

Current response, masked response, complete-case response, validity-only and
the probing control have identical expected test quality here. Neutral
retention attains the same quality with lower money and measured evaluation
time. The additional money/time-budget view also shows no surviving advantage.
Its allocation integration is numerical; all 560 allocations are conditional
rearrangements of the same recorded matrix, not new model trials.

The tiny test differences should not be described as statistically established
quality superiority. For example, current response minus structural retention
is **+0.000305 percentage points**, with an approximate paired workload-bank
95% interval **[-0.01985, +0.02045]**. Such intervals describe fresh workload
sampling conditional on this descendant matrix; they do not cover uncertainty
over independent LLM search banks. The stronger negative evidence is that the
extra information fails to separate response selection from its cheap or
information-ignoring controls.

**3. Do invalid probes explain the apparent diversity?** They explain a major
part of the original retention decision. Of the squared distance making `p06`
the first diverse representative, **99.945% comes from the artificial invalid
coordinate**. Its total distance from the best seed is 0.500276, versus 0.005913
for the next parent. Replacing the invalid penalty with 0 or 1 percentage point
drops `p06`; penalties of 10, 100 and 1,000 retain it. Masking missing responses
or requiring complete valid pairs also drops it. The penalty sweep was a
diagnostic, not a search for a favorable test score.

The fresh bank gives `p06` **zero** children beating the initial global training
best. The sole fresh global training improvement is instead
[new_p01_02](../../experiments/metamuse_continuation/calls/a030/program.py), from `p01`,
which ordinary performance retention already keeps. Thus fixing the invalidity
encoding changes parent identity but does not supply useful new predictive
power in this comparison. A differing sampled mutation still does not prove
unequal underlying LLM kernels.

**4. Can useful search information be obtained more cheaply?** The same old
response-selected parent set can be obtained with the eight local-edit probes
instead of all sixteen probes. At 32 credits this raises the conditional
probability of reaching the fresh training winner from **1/2 to 3/4**, because
more budget remains for future edits. Performance-only retention reaches it
with probability **1**, without buying response information. The reduced probe
assay is therefore a cost ablation, not evidence of an efficient new method.
Useful incremental information beyond the free controls remains unestablished.

Fresh evaluation also weakens the initial candidate-quality story. On these
96 traces, Best Fit scores **6.793143**, the fresh training winner **6.793752**,
and the old winner, scored only as a reference, **6.800138**. Each candidate
wins one trace, loses one and ties 94 against Best Fit. Their paired workload-
bank intervals include zero. The earlier improvement on two of twelve test
traces has not replicated as an average gain on this fresh set. These are
percentage-excess-over-volume-bound costs, not percentage reductions in bin count.

**5. Is a scientifically distinctive new selection mechanism justified?** No.
Masked missing data, validity filtering, a one-label assay and ordinary archive
retention are useful controls, not established innovations. These results
support neither a new bin-packing algorithm nor a new selection algorithm.
They do not identify a performance-equivalence bug in MetaMuse. This code is
an independent MetaMuse-inspired refinement adapter, not an official reproduction.
The existing novelty limitations remain applicable.

**6. Is further experimentation or a Francis Yan follow-up worthwhile?**
**NO-GO for further paid tuning on this parent bank and for a new-method pitch.**
Do not rescue a selector by modifying these held-out workloads or choosing a
favorable penalty. A worthwhile next input would be independent creative-parent
and offspring trajectories or materially different parent banks. A factual
discussion or archive-focused inquiry could be reasonable; the current evidence
does not warrant presenting an innovation. No email or Technical Note was drafted.
